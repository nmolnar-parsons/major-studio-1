## Sketch 1: Color by Year

![Sketch 1](https://github.com/nmolnar-parsons/major-studio-1/blob/main/Project_2/Sketches/Proj2_sketch1.jpeg)

Arrange/group portraits by year of creation. Use Vibrant.Js to extract prominent colors and find the most common shade (may need to do some nudging of colors here -> i.e. if there's a lot of slightly different yellows, most prominent should be "yellow"). For each year, get the most prominent color, and add it to a gradient bar of colors that can be scrolled forward in time from 1780 to 1810. Unscroll/unveil the gallery as the user scrolls


## Sketch 2

![Sketch 2](https://github.com/nmolnar-parsons/major-studio-1/blob/main/Project_2/Sketches/Proj2_sketch2.jpeg)

Place portraits in their historical contexts and the lives of their sitters. These portraits were painted at multiple different points in sitter's lifetimes, and are more interesting when placed in their historical or personal contexts. This sketch is a very rough example of a scrollable timeline from 1780 to 1810. Portraits are "hung" from timelines of the sitter's life. Death-dates are marked for sitters who died between 1780 and 1810. The timelines will not be annotated, but contextual historical information will be added to each portrait. The main timeline will be annotated with larger historical events. Users will be able to select specific sitters/groups of sitters, but the chart will start with a pre-loaded example.

For more space, I'm thinking of switching the scroll to the vertical axis.
