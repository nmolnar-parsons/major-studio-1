# Project 3 Concepts and Sketches

## Concept 1: "Portrait Wordle"

![Sketch1](https://github.com/nmolnar-parsons/major-studio-1/blob/main/Project_3/Sketches/Sketch1.png)

A wordle-style guessing game of faces present in the Smithsonian dataset. In Five guesses, the player must figure out who they are looking at. After each guess, more information about the face will be revealed. Information is revealed in the following order: (1) face, (2) full portrait, (3) year, (4) Occupation, (5) Number of Portraits.
By playing several rounds of the game, the player will become more familiar with the people present in this collection.

## Concept 2: "Face Matching Category" (in lieu of a better name)

![Sketch2](https://github.com/nmolnar-parsons/major-studio-1/blob/main/Project_3/Sketches/Sketch2.png)

Match portraits in a 3x3 grid based on their sitter and properties. In the example above, players match the sitter with the material type of the portrait. Categories could change - could match sitters with date, artist, occupation. Players would become familiar with different faces and different aspects of the portraits in the collection.


## Concept 3: Cabinet of Features

![Sketch3](https://github.com/nmolnar-parsons/major-studio-1/blob/main/Project_3/Sketches/Sketch3.png)

Isolate features from faces - specifically eyes, but hopefully mouthes, noses, and potentially hair. Players would then reconstruct portraits using the features available.
